Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19)

COVID-19 is caused by SARS-CoV-2. It spreads through droplets released when you breathe, cough, sneeze, or speak, and it can be severe.

This page is a short summary. Full guidance is on the New York State COVID-19 page (opens in a new tab).
Orange County programs are on the county COVID-19 page (opens in a new tab).

COVID-19 guidance

Symptoms

COVID-19 often feels like a cold, the flu, or pneumonia. Common signs include the following; however, some people have no symptoms.

  • Fever
  • Cough
  • Shortness of breath
  • Sore throat
  • Fatigue
  • New loss of taste or smell

For more information go to Prevention Symptoms (opens in a new tab).

Higher risk

Older adults (65 and older), people who are not up to date on vaccination, and people with certain medical conditions, such as chronic lung disease, heart disease, or a weakened immune system, are more likely to get very sick. Antiviral treatment should start within days of the first symptoms. Who is at higher risk (opens in a new tab)

Protect yourself

Stay up to date on COVID-19 vaccines, wash your hands, avoid close contact with people who are sick, and stay home when you have symptoms. Prevention and symptoms (opens in a new tab)

Testing and treatment

A viral test (PCR or antigen) looks for a current infection. If you have symptoms, test right away. People at higher risk should contact a healthcare provider promptly. Antivirals are not a substitute for vaccination. Testing (opens in a new tab) · Treatment (opens in a new tab)

Vaccines

Staying up to date lowers the risk of severe illness, hospitalization, and death. Vaccines are available at pharmacies, doctor’s offices, local health departments, and clinics. COVID-19 vaccine (opens in a new tab)

When to seek emergency help

  • Trouble breathing
  • Pain or pressure in the chest that does not go away
  • Confusion or trouble waking up
  • Bluish lips or face

If someone has any of these signs, call 911.
